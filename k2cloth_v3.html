About K–2

A Legacy Built on Quality & Commitment

K–2 Cloth was founded in 1990 by Mrs Kamal Kapoor and formally established at SIDCUL Industrial Area, Haridwar in 2005 by Mr Himesh Kapoor.

Today K–2 operates two divisions — K–2 Cloth for uniforms & auto canopy, and K–2 Industries for premium chair upholstery & core paper tubes — united by precision and on-time delivery.

Mission

To fulfill the quality and commitments of our valuable customers on time, every time.

Vision

To adopt the latest technology and become a prime industry leader in stitching.
